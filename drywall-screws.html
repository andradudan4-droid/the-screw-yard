Drywall Screws

Drywall screws fix plasterboard to timber or metal stud. Fine thread grips light gauge metal, coarse thread bites into timber, and collated strips feed an auto-feed screw gun for faster boarding.

This range has 5 lines: Fine Thread Drywall Screw, Coarse Thread Drywall Screw, Bugle Head Self-Drilling Screw, Collated Drywall Screw and Collated Drywall Self-Drilling Screw.
