Self-Tapping Screws

Self-tapping screws have a sharp point and cut their own thread into a pilot hole in sheet metal, plastic or timber. Head and drive styles vary by line and are listed below.

This range has 7 lines: Countersunk Head Self-Tapping Screw, Pan Head Self-Tapping Screw, Flat Head Self-Tapping Screw, Modified Truss Head Self-Tapping Screw, Pan Framing Head Self-Tapping Screw, Hex Head Self-Tapping Screw and Pan Head Self-Tapping Screw.
