Wood & Timber Screws

Screws for timber, chipboard and sheet materials. Countersunk chipboard screws cover most joinery and carcassing, coach screws take heavier structural fixings, and confirmat screws suit flat-pack and cabinet work.

This range has 8 lines: Countersunk Chipboard Screw, Cross Recess Flat Head Wood Screw, Hexagon Flange Wood Screw, Hexagon Head Wood Screw (Coach Screw), Slotted Pan Head Wood Screw, Confirmat Screw, Wafer Head Timber Screw and Coloured Wood Screw.
