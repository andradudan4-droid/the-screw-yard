Nails

Loose nails for framing, roofing, masonry and finishing work. Finishes depend on the line: polished, electro galvanised or hot dip galvanised.

This range has 12 lines: Common Nails, Concrete Nails, Umbrella Head Roofing Nails, Assembled Roofing Nails, Finishing Nails, Clout Nails, Cut Masonry Nails, U Nails (Staples), Headless Nails, Duplex Nails, Spiral Spike Nails and Large Head Roofing Nails.

Sold by the box. Free delivery on 5 boxes or more. Prices are shown excluding and including VAT where listed. For everything else, send us your list and we will come back with a price.
