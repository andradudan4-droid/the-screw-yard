Questions

The things people ask us most. Anything else, give us a ring on 07958 146 847.

How do I order?

Tap + on the products and sizes you need to build your basket, then send the list to us on WhatsApp. We confirm the price and stock, and you pay once you have accepted. There is no checkout and no account to make. You can also just call us on 07958 146 847.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free on orders over £50 including VAT. For smaller orders we tell you the delivery charge before you pay.

Do your prices include VAT?

We show every price both including and excluding VAT at 20%, so trade and DIY customers can see the figure they need.

How many screws are in a 1 kg box?

It depends on the size: the bigger the screw, the fewer in a kilo. As a rough guide, a 1 kg box holds about 73 chipboard screws at 6.0 x 120 mm, about 159 at 5.0 x 80 mm and about 345 at 4.0 x 50 mm. Ask us if you need an exact count.

Do you take trade and bulk orders?

Yes. Trade and bulk orders are welcome. Send us your list with the quantities and we will come back with a price.

I can't find the size or fixing I need.

Products without a price are priced on request, and we can source more than what is listed. Tell us the job, size, finish and quantity and we will find the fixing.

Which screw should I use?

Our fixing guides cover plasterboard, decking, concrete and brick, and screw sizes. If the fixing is structural or safety critical, check with a qualified person first.
