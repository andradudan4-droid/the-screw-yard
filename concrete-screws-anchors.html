Concrete Screws & Anchors

Fixings for concrete, brick and block. Concrete screws thread straight into a drilled hole, while wedge, drop-in and nylon anchors expand in the hole to hold heavier loads.

This range has 10 lines: Flat Head Concrete Screw, Blue Concrete Screw, Hex Head Concrete Screw Bolt, Drop-In Anchor, Expansion Anchor Bolt, Cut Anchor, Wedge Anchor, Nylon Anchor with Screw, Plastic Wall Plugs and Shooting Nails with Washer.
