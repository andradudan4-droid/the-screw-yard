Fixing guides

Plain answers to the questions we get asked most, with sizes and tables you can use on the job.

  • Which screw for plasterboard?Into timber, use a coarse thread drywall screw: 38 mm for 12.5 mm board. Into metal stud, use a fine thread drywall screw: 25 mm for 12.5 mm board.
  • What size screws for decking?Use a screw 4.5 to 5 mm in diameter and at least two and a half times as long as the board is thick. For a 28 mm deck board that means 65 to 70 mm.
  • Self-tapping vs self-drilling screwsA self-tapping screw cuts its own thread but needs a pilot hole in metal. A self-drilling screw has a drill point, so it makes the hole and the thread in one go.
  • How to fix into concrete, brick and blockLight loads: a plug and screw. Medium loads: a concrete screw, straight into a drilled hole. Heavy loads in solid concrete: a wedge anchor.
  • Screw sizes explained: gauge and lengthA metric screw size is diameter x length in millimetres. A 5 x 80 screw is 5 mm across the thread and 80 mm long.
