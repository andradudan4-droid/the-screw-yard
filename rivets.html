Rivets & Rivet Nuts

Blind rivets are set from one side of the work with a rivet gun. The range covers standard, large flange and sealed waterproof types, plus rivet nuts for adding a thread to thin sheet.

This range has 4 lines: Blind Rivet, Large Flange Blind Rivet, Waterproof Blind Rivet and Rivet Nuts.

Sold by the box. Free delivery on 5 boxes or more. Prices are shown excluding and including VAT where listed. For everything else, send us your list and we will come back with a price.
