Self-Drilling Screws

Self-drilling screws have a drill point that makes its own hole in steel, so there is no pilot hole to drill. They are used for cladding, roofing sheets, steel framing and fixing timber to metal.

This range has 13 lines: Countersunk Head Self-Drilling Screw, Countersunk Ribbed Head Self-Drilling Screw, Countersunk Head Self-Drilling Screw with Wings, Modified Truss Head Self-Drilling Screw, Pan Head Self-Drilling Screw, Pan Framing Serrated Self-Drilling Screw, Hex Head Self-Drilling Screw, Hex Head Self-Drilling Screw with Washer, Window Screw, Sandwich Panel Screw, Colour Head Roofing Screw, Hex Head Roofing Screw with Colour Cap and Colour Head Self-Drilling Screw.

Sold by the box. Free delivery on 5 boxes or more. Prices are shown excluding and including VAT where listed. For everything else, send us your list and we will come back with a price.
