Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

The short version: this website has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising trackers. We only get your details if you choose to call or message us.

1. Who is responsible for your data

CMD Team Ltd, trading as The Screw Yard, is the data controller. Contact us about privacy on 07958 146 847.

2. What we collect and why

When you browse

We do not collect anything that identifies you. Your quote list is saved in your own browser, on your device. It is not sent to us unless you choose to send it. See our cookies page.

When you call or message us

If you phone us or send your list by WhatsApp, we receive your phone number, your name if you give it, and whatever you tell us about what you need. We use this to reply, prepare your quote and supply your order.

Our legal basis is taking steps you have asked for before entering into a contract and, for general enquiries, our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us.

3. Who else is involved

  • Vercel hosts this website. Like any web host it processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which means Google receives your IP address. Google says it does not use this to profile or advertise to you.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages you choose to send us through it, under its own privacy policy.

Some of these companies process data outside the UK. They rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law. We never sell your data or pass it to anyone for marketing.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as we need to deal with them. If you buy from us, we keep order and invoice records for up to six years after the sale, as tax law requires.

5. Marketing

We do not send marketing messages unless you have asked us to. You can tell us to stop at any time.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct it if it is wrong
  • delete it
  • stop or limit how we use it
  • send it to you or someone else in a portable format

To use any of these rights, contact us on 07958 146 847. We will reply within one month and it is free.

7. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Changes

We will update this page if what we do with data changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
